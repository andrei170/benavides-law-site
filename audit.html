You are winning the easy searches and losing the valuable ones. When someone searches "Cynthia Benavides" or "Weslaco probate lawyer," you show up. The 6 highest-impact fixes

Quick win · week 1

1Rewrite your title tags and meta descriptions

Change the homepage title to something like "Estate Planning & Probate Attorney in Weslaco, TX | Law Office of Cynthia Benavides." This single change tells Google what you do and where, and it is the highest-leverage hour of work on the whole site.

Foundation · weeks 1–3

2Build a real page for each practice area

Turn each short overview into a full page that answers the real questions: what a Lady Bird deed is, how Texas probate works, what business succession involves. 800–1,200 words each. This is what earns rankings and builds trust before the first call.

Growth · weeks 2–6

3Add city pages for the Valley

One page each for McAllen, Edinburg, Pharr, Mission, Harlingen and Brownsville. This is exactly how the firms that outrank you cover the whole Valley while you only rank in Weslaco.

Authority · ongoing

4Start a simple, useful blog

Two short articles a month answering common questions ("Do I need a will if I have a Lady Bird deed?", "What happens if you die without a will in Texas?"). These rank, get cited by AI assistants, and bring in clients on autopilot.

Technical · week 1

5Add structured data (schema)

Add LegalService, Attorney and FAQ schema so Google and AI tools like ChatGPT understand your firm, your location and your services. The sample site we built already includes this, ready to use.

Local · week 1

6Optimize your Google Business Profile

Fully complete the profile, add services and photos, and steadily gather reviews. For local law searches, the map pack often sits above the regular results, and it is the fastest source of new calls.
