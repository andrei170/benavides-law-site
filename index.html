Common Questions

Estate planning & probate, answered

What is a Lady Bird deed and do I need one?

A Lady Bird (enhanced life estate) deed lets you keep full control of your property during your lifetime and pass it automatically to your heirs at death, without probate. For many Valley homeowners it is one of the simplest, lowest-cost ways to protect the family home. We will tell you whether it fits your situation.

How much does an estate plan cost?

It depends on what you need, from a simple will and deed to a full trust-based plan. We offer flat fees for most estate work and a $100 consultation for non-injury matters so you know the cost up front. Personal injury consultations are always free.

Do I have to go through probate in Texas?

Not always. With the right planning, many estates avoid full probate through tools like Lady Bird deeds, Transfer-on-Death deeds and trusts. If a loved one has already passed, we will walk you through the simplest probate path available, such as a muniment of title.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. We serve clients in both English and Spanish so your family fully understands every document they sign.

What does a personal injury case cost me?

Nothing up front. Injury consultations are free, and we work on contingency, which means you pay no attorney fee unless we recover money for you.
