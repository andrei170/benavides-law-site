Your site does a real job of telling people who you are. What it does not do is capture the ones who are not ready to pick up the phone today, and that is most of them. Here is exactly where the Law Office of Cynthia Benavides leaks clients, and what it is worth to seal it.

Your site today

We looked at benavideslawfirmweslaco.com the way a prospective client would. Here is what is working, and what is quietly costing you.

What you've got

  • A clean site that explains your five practice areas
  • A "Request a Consultation" contact form
  • Phone and email shown clearly
  • A free consultation for injury cases

What's leaking

  • No lead magnet, nothing for the researcher who isn't ready to call
  • No online booking, they have to call and wait for a callback
  • No automatic email or text follow-up on enquiries
  • No instant response, so slower than the next firm they message
The form and phone catch the small slice of people ready to act right now. Everyone else, the ones comparing two or three firms or just starting to plan, has no low-risk way to raise their hand, and no reason to come back. That is the leak.
